## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor, mandatory, owned by the methodology seat): From an editorial-oversight standpoint, this dimension asks whether the research design, data treatment, statistical reporting and the means for others to reproduce the work reach the level a peer-reviewed empirical venue expects. It is scored by the methodology seat, not by me; I will only note editorially whether reproducibility material is signposted.

D2 (domain_accuracy, mandatory, owned by the domain seat): This dimension checks that claims agree with the current body of domain evidence, that prior studies are represented faithfully, and that field-specific terminology and results are stated without factual error. It belongs to the domain seat.

D3 (argumentative_coherence, mandatory, owned by the devil's advocate with the methodology seat also eligible): This dimension asks whether the central thesis holds together, whether the evidence presented actually carries the claims, and whether any reasoning fallacy undermines the main argument. It is outside my scoring remit.

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance, high priority, owned by the perspective seat): This dimension asks whether framing, definitions and implications are readable by researchers in neighbouring fields and whether any interdisciplinary claims are properly substantiated. It is outside my scoring remit.

D5 (writing_and_structure, normal priority, owned by eic): This dimension, which I score, concerns how the manuscript is organised, how clearly it is written, the quality and self-sufficiency of its tables and figures, and whether it follows the conventions of the configured venue (here a Springer journal, so Springer house style for sections, declarations, references, tables and figures). Because it is normal priority, it can move a decision toward minor revision but cannot by itself trigger a fatal outcome.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution, mandatory, owned by eic): This dimension, which I also score, asks whether the manuscript belongs in the configured venue, whose stated scope in the contract requires empirical analysis of Asia-Pacific financial data, and whether it offers an original and significant contribution relative to what that readership already knows. As a mandatory dimension, a block here drives major revision and a fatal block drives rejection under the contract's failure conditions.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure
dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: Logical section ordering (introduction, literature, data, method, results, robustness, conclusion), a clear statement of contribution early on, consistent notation and correctly rendered equations, tables and figures that are numbered, captioned, referenced in text and interpretable without the body, and Springer conventions (structured abstract length, keywords, JEL codes, declarations such as funding, competing interests and data availability, author-year or numbered references applied consistently).
what_triggers_block: Organisation or exposition so deficient that a reader cannot locate the research question, method or principal results, or core tables and figures are missing, unreadable, mislabelled or contradict the text such that results cannot be verified from the presentation.
what_triggers_warn: Localised clarity problems, inconsistent notation or terminology, tables or figures lacking units, sample periods, captions or source notes, uneven section balance, excessive length relative to content, or missing or incomplete Springer-required elements such as declarations, keywords, JEL codes or reference-style consistency.

### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution
dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: Whether the empirical core uses Asia-Pacific financial market data as the contract's venue note requires, whether the research question matters to that journal's readership, whether the contribution is explicitly positioned against the closest prior work, and whether the claimed novelty is original and of practical or scholarly significance rather than a routine replication.
what_triggers_block: The contribution is stated but not differentiated from closest prior studies, or its significance for the venue's readership is asserted without support, or the Asia-Pacific empirical component is present but secondary to the paper's main thrust, such that substantial repositioning is needed before the venue could consider it.
what_triggers_warn: The contribution is real and in scope but under-articulated, with novelty claims that are somewhat overstated, thin discussion of implications for regional investors, regulators or researchers, or limited engagement with recent literature published in or near the venue.
what_triggers_fatal: The manuscript contains no empirical analysis of Asia-Pacific financial data, falls outside the venue's scope entirely, or offers no identifiable original contribution beyond reproducing existing published results.
