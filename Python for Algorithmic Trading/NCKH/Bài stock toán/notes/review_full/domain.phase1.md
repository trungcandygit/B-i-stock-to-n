## Scoring Plan

### D2: domain_accuracy
dimension_id: D2
what_to_look_for: Correct representation of foundational and recent literature on emerging-market and ASEAN equity co-movement, detrended cross-correlation and multifractal methods, and volatility-bias-adjusted correlation; accurate institutional facts about the studied market (exchanges, index constituents and construction rules, trading bands, foreign-ownership limits, trading-hour and settlement conventions); precise terminology; and results claims that are consistent with established domain evidence or explicitly reconciled with it.
what_triggers_block: A material factual error about index construction, market institutions, or regulation that changes the interpretation of a headline result, or a misrepresentation of a key prior study or method that the central claim depends on, which is correctable through revision.
what_triggers_warn: Omission of important seminal or recent domain literature, imprecise or inconsistent domain terminology, or minor institutional inaccuracies and unreconciled divergences from prior evidence that do not overturn the headline conclusions.
what_triggers_fatal: The central claim rests on a fundamental domain misconception, such as mischaracterising the mechanical relationship between the indices studied or the meaning of the correlation measures, such that no revision of the stated design can make the core conclusion valid.
