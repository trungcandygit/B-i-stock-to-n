## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor): From an adversarial stance, this dimension asks whether the design, data handling, statistical reporting and reproducibility material would hold up against a sceptical referee who assumes every undocumented choice was made to favour the result. It is mandatory, and only the methodology seat scores it. I will raise challenges here only as findings, not as a score.

D2 (domain_accuracy): This dimension asks whether the domain claims, the way prior work is represented and the technical terminology would survive a specialist trying to catch misattribution, misreading of earlier results or misuse of terms. It is mandatory and belongs to the domain seat. I report counter-evidence here only as findings, with no score.

D3 (argumentative_coherence): This is my scored dimension and it is mandatory. It asks whether the core thesis stays consistent from premise to conclusion, whether the evidence actually supports the stated claims rather than a weaker or different claim, and whether any fallacy, hidden assumption, overlooked rival explanation or overgeneralisation beyond the evidence undermines the central argument. I will stress-test the logic chain, build the strongest counter-narrative and check whether the conclusions go further than the analysis allows.

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance): This dimension asks whether framing, definitions and implications can be read by researchers in adjacent fields, and whether any interdisciplinary claim is substantiated rather than asserted. It is high priority and belongs to the perspective seat. I challenge it only through findings.

D5 (writing_and_structure): This dimension covers organisation, clarity of exposition, figure and table quality and venue conventions. It is normal priority and the EIC seat owns it. I will not score it; presentation issues enter my report only if they hide or distort the argument.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution): This dimension asks whether the manuscript fits the configured venue and makes an original, significant contribution for its readers. It is mandatory and the EIC seat owns it. My "so what?" challenges may appear as findings, but I do not score venue fit.

## Scoring Plan

### D3: argumentative_coherence
dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Whether each inferential step from the stated premises and evidence to the headline conclusion is valid; whether key terms keep one meaning throughout; whether rival explanations (mechanical or construction artefacts, confounding, sample or period specificity) are named and ruled out; and whether the scope of the conclusions stays within what the evidence covers.
what_triggers_block: The headline conclusion does not follow from the presented evidence, or a plausible rival explanation that fits the same evidence at least as well is left unaddressed, but the defect can be repaired through re-analysis, reframing or narrowing the claim.
what_triggers_warn: Localised coherence problems: an unstated hidden assumption, a term used inconsistently, a secondary claim stated more strongly than its evidence, or generalisation beyond the studied setting. The core argument survives each one.
what_triggers_fatal: The manuscript's own reported evidence directly contradicts its central thesis, or the central claim rests on circular reasoning that makes it true by construction, so that no revision short of abandoning the core thesis could restore it.
