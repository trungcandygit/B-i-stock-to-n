## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor, mandatory, owned by the methodology seat): the editor needs assurance that the design, data handling, statistical reporting and reproducibility materials would satisfy a referee who specialises in the method. From an editorial standpoint this is the dimension most likely to decide whether the paper can be sent out at all, but its scoring belongs to the methodology seat, not to me.

D2 (domain_accuracy, mandatory, owned by the domain seat): claims must agree with the current evidence base, prior work must be represented fairly, and domain terms and results must be used correctly. Editorially, misrepresented prior work undermines any novelty claim, but the domain seat scores it.

D3 (argumentative_coherence, mandatory, owned by the devil's advocate with methodology also eligible): the central thesis must hold together, the evidence must carry the claims, and no fallacy may undercut the main argument. I note coherence problems only where they bear on fit and contribution; I do not score this dimension.

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance, high priority, owned by the perspective seat): framing, definitions and implications must be accessible to readers from adjacent fields, and interdisciplinary claims must be substantiated. Not mine to score.

D5 (writing_and_structure, normal priority, owned by the EIC): the manuscript's organisation, clarity, figure and table quality, and adherence to the conventions of the venue class must allow a reader to follow and verify the argument without undue effort. I score this.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution, mandatory, owned by the EIC): the manuscript must fit the configured venue (here only a venue class, since no specific journal is bound) and make an original and significant contribution suited to that readership. This is my primary dimension and the one where a fatal finding would mean the paper should not be considered by this class of outlet in any revised form.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure
dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: Title, abstract, introduction, results and conclusion tell one consistent story; contribution statements are explicit and match what is delivered; sections follow the venue-class article structure; each table and figure is labelled, numbered in citation order, readable, and necessary; length and exhibit count are proportionate to the contribution; peripheral material is in an appendix rather than the main text.
what_triggers_block: The exposition is disorganised enough that a referee cannot reconstruct the main result from the main text, or key exhibits are missing, unreadable or inconsistent with the text such that verification is impossible without author help.
what_triggers_warn: The structure is sound but the paper is materially longer or more exhibit-heavy than its contribution warrants, peripheral analyses crowd the main line, figure or table numbering or labelling is inconsistent, or the abstract and conclusion state the findings more strongly or differently than the results sections.

### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution
dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: A clearly stated economic or scientific question that readers of the venue class care about; an originality source (new data, method, theory, or a demonstrated insight) that is distinguished from the nearest prior work; a significance argument beyond the single case studied; consistency between the claimed contributions and the hypotheses actually supported; framing aimed at one identifiable readership; a result that is not simply a restatement of a known or trivially derivable property.
what_triggers_block: The core contribution is substantially a known or trivially derivable result presented as new, or the paper does not establish why the venue-class readership needs it, but a repositioning, reframing or added evidence could plausibly fix this in one major revision.
what_triggers_warn: The contribution is real but under-positioned against the nearest precedents, the evidence base is narrow relative to the generality claimed, part of the listed contributions rests on unsupported hypotheses, or the framing oscillates between readerships so that fit is uncertain.
what_triggers_fatal: The manuscript has no defensible original contribution for the venue class even after reframing, because its central result is already established in prior work or follows by construction with no empirical or conceptual content left once that is acknowledged, or its topic lies outside the venue class's scope.
