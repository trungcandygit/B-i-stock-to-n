## Theme 1 — DCCA / ρDCCA / MF-DCCA

### 1. Okorie & Lin (2021)

## Theme 2 — Multiscale correlation and portfolio / diversification

## Theme 4 — Index composition and membership-induced comovement

### 17. Barberis, Shleifer & Wurgler (2005) [CLASSIC, pre-2021]
Barberis, N., Shleifer, A., & Wurgler, J. (2005). Comovement. *Journal of Financial Economics, 75*(2), 283–317. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2004.04.003
- Finding: after a stock is added to the S&P 500, its beta with the index rises, and the rise is larger once non-S&P returns are controlled for. This supports friction- and sentiment-based comovement over the fundamentals-based view.
- Verified at: https://shleifer.scholars.harvard.edu/publications/comovement and https://dash.harvard.edu/entities/publication/73120378-fcfd-6bd4-e053-0100007fdf3b
- Verified fields: authors, year, title, journal, volume 75, issue 2, pages 283–317, DOI.

### 18. Liao, Coakley & Kellard (2022)
Liao, Y., Coakley, J., & Kellard, N. (2022). Index tracking and beta arbitrage effects in comovement. *International Review of Financial Analysis, 83*, 102330. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.irfa.2022.102330
- Finding: for S&P 500 additions, common demand from indexers and beta arbitrageurs drives beta increases in low-beta stocks. Arbitrageur shorting dampens or reverses the increases for high-beta additions.
- Verified at: https://repository.essex.ac.uk/33243/
- Verified fields: authors, year, title, journal, volume 83, article 102330, DOI.

### 19. Greenwood & Sammon (2025)
Greenwood, R., & Sammon, M. (2025). The disappearing index effect. *The Journal of Finance, 80*(2), 657–698. https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.13410
- Finding: abnormal returns on S&P 500 additions fell from 7.4% in the 1990s to 0.3% in the most recent decade, and deletions show a similar pattern, even though index-linked assets have grown.
- Verified at: https://ideas.repec.org/a/bla/jfinan/v80y2025i2p657-698.html
- Verified fields: authors, year, title, journal, volume 80, issue 2, pages 657–698, DOI.

### 20. DeCoste (2025)
DeCoste, J. (2025). Comovement and S&P 500 membership. *Global Finance Journal, 65*, 101110. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.gfj.2025.101110

- Verified at: https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/glofin/v65y2025ics1044028325000377.html and https://blogs.unb.ca/ideas-with-impact/2025/04/joseph-decoste.php
