## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor): From a domain-accuracy standpoint, this dimension asks whether the design, data handling, statistical reporting and reproducibility support meet the peer-review bar of empirical finance and financial econometrics. It is owned by the methodology seat; I will not score it, and I will only flag domain consequences of design choices (for example, whether a data construction changes the economic meaning of a quantity) inside my prose.

D2 (domain_accuracy): This is my owned dimension. It asks whether claims are consistent with the current body of evidence in co-movement, index-membership, diversification and contagion research; whether prior work is represented faithfully (including precedents that bear on any novelty claim); whether terminology (correlation, contagion, interdependence, co-movement, mechanical vs economic components, institutional rules) is used as the field uses it; and whether institutional and market-specific facts are correct.

D3 (argumentative_coherence): This dimension asks whether the central thesis holds together, whether the evidence actually supports each stated claim, and whether no fallacy (circularity, equivocation, overreach) undermines the main argument. It is owned by the devil's advocate and shared with methodology; I do not score it, but where the economic interpretation of results relies on an inference the literature does not license, I will describe that in domain terms.

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance): This dimension asks whether framing, definitions and implications are accessible and substantiated for readers in adjacent fields (econophysics, risk practice, index design). It belongs to the perspective seat; I do not score it.

D5 (writing_and_structure): This dimension covers organisation, clarity, figure and table quality and venue conventions. It belongs to the journal-fit seat; I do not score it.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution): This dimension asks whether the manuscript fits the configured venue and makes an original, significant contribution for that readership. It belongs to the journal-fit seat; I do not score it, although my assessment of whether the novelty claim survives a search of prior work will be reported as domain evidence the synthesizer may use.

## Scoring Plan

### D2: domain_accuracy
dimension_id: D2
what_to_look_for: Faithful representation of the co-movement, index-membership, part-whole correlation, holdings-overlap and interdependence-versus-contagion literatures; a novelty claim that survives a search of finance and statistics precedents; hypotheses grounded in named theory; interpretations of results consistent with established mechanisms; correct institutional and regulatory facts for the studied market; correct use of field terminology.
what_triggers_block: A headline novelty or gap claim is contradicted by identifiable published precedent that the manuscript does not cite or distinguish, or a core economic interpretation of the main result conflicts with established domain evidence without acknowledgement, requiring substantial repositioning or rewriting.
what_triggers_warn: Key references or rival explanations are missing, some prior work or institutional facts are imprecise or dated, terminology is occasionally loose, or hypotheses are linked to theory only superficially, but the core claims stand after repair.
what_triggers_fatal: The central contribution is shown to be already established in prior literature in substantively the same form so that no defensible original domain contribution remains, or the main conclusions rest on a factual or conceptual domain error that cannot be corrected without invalidating them.
